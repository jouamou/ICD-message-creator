# ICD Message Creator

Make an ICD card for a colleague in under a minute: thanks, celebrations, occasions (Ramadan, Eid, Hajj) and condolences, in English or Arabic, in the new ICD identity.

**Open it:** https://jouamou.github.io/ICD-message-creator/

## How it works

1. **Choose the occasion.** 23 ready messages in four groups: Thank you, Celebrate, Occasions and Care.
2. **Make it yours.** Edit the title and message, add who it is for, your name and your job title. Arabic is set right to left automatically.
3. **Choose the look.** Three sizes (Card for email and Teams, Square for WhatsApp and LinkedIn, Story for status updates), seven designs drawn from the logo's petals, bands and pentagon, and the brand colours. Condolence and care messages only offer the quiet designs, without gold.
4. **Send it.** Download a sharp PNG (twice the screen size), copy the image straight into Teams or Outlook, copy the text, or share from a phone.

## Privacy

Everything runs in the browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere: the page makes no network requests, and the logo and Arabic font are built into the file. Your name and job title are remembered only in your own browser.

## Notes

- `index.html` is generated. It embeds the approved logo vectors and the Noto Kufi Arabic font (SIL Open Font License).
- The new Arabic messages (Thank you, Welcome, Farewell, Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Hajj) still need a check by a native Arabic reader.
